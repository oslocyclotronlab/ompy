# Glossary

- **Level density** — Number of energy levels per energy interval, typically
  expressed in MeV⁻¹. Central to normalization and model tuning.
- **γ-strength function** — Probability distribution for γ-ray emission; used in
  normalization and decomposition steps.
- **Unfolding** — Process of removing detector-response effects from measured
  spectra to recover true physical distributions.
- **First-generation spectrum** — Primary γ-ray yield after subtracting
  higher-order cascades from coincidence data.
